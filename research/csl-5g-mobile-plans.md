**Primary URL:** [csl 5G landing](https://5g.hkcsl.com/5g/index.html?&redirectURL=https%3A%2F%2Fshop.hkcsl.com%2FMobOs%2Flist.html%3Fnature%3DACQ%26plantype%3Dsimplan%26language%3Dzh%26lang%3Dzh)

Tavily (requested as “Travily”) was **not available** in this environment (no Tavily MCP tool and no API key). This summary is based on the landing/shop pages above plus official csl 5G tariff and related plan pages.

All prices below are **HKD**. A **HK$18 / month MTR / tunnels / mobile licence / administration fee** applies to the contract plans unless a page says otherwise. Offers are typically for **new activations or recontracts** with a **24- or 36-month commitment**. Early termination is charged as the remaining monthly fees.

### Included with the standard 5G plans

- Local Wi-Fi at 15,000+ csl hotspots
- 500 intra-network SMS, 200 MMS, 10 international SMS
- Voicemail, call forwarding / hold / waiting, conference call, caller ID, call filtering
- 2,000 Clubpoints on signup (The Club); extra 500 Clubpoints per additional SIM
- SafetyNet (12 months complimentary on designated plans)
- Now TV app and MOOV local data waiver
- Viu Premium Lite during the commitment period on designated plans (stated value $38 / month)
- 2-month Restart 3.0 handset-switch trial on designated **in-store** signups (then $58 / month)

Roaming data in the 3 GB Mainland China & Macau pack does **not** carry over. Unused local quota generally does not carry over unless a “piggy bank” add-on applies (80 GB+ plans).

## Network and usage rules (apply across 5G plans)

- 5G only in designated coverage with a compatible device; otherwise 4.5G / 3G.
- Actual speed is below headline network specs and depends on device, location, and congestion.
- Tethering and P2P (including BitTorrent) are allowed on selected entitlements; **only one SIM** in a multi-SIM group may tether / P2P at a time.
- 5G roaming needs IDD + roaming + data-roaming pass, a supported device, and a supported visited network.
- After high-speed quota, most postpaid 5G plans **throttle** rather than hard-stop; student 80 GB U-Plan is an exception (service stops until next bill cycle).

## How to read the prices

1. **Add $18 / month** admin fee to contract plans when comparing total bill.
2. **SIM-only online promo** (the URL you asked about) currently clusters around **$138 / 30 GB**, **$168 / 60 GB**, **$198 / 100 GB**.
3. **Handset contracts** use higher list fees with in-contract rebates (net **$208–$598** for 60–500 GB).
4. **Youth (Go! Chill)** under 29: **$158 / 30 GB** and **$188 / 60 GB** with extra roaming day-passes.
5. **Cross-border (中港澳)** starts at **$298** with shared Greater China data, not just a small 3 GB roaming pack.
6. Prices and SKUs change often; confirm on the [live SIM-only list](https://shop.hkcsl.com/MobOs/list.html?nature=ACQ&plantype=simplan&language=zh&lang=zh) before purchase.
